## Part 1 · Set up the cube

[▶ Watch this part (0:11)](https://youtu.be/lxKNAoFo2Wk?t=11)

| Sidebar → Dimensions | Value |
| --- | --- |
| X | 1.5 m |
| Y | 1.5 m |
| Z | 6 m |

1. Open Blender and click **General** to start a new file. You see a grey cube in the middle.
2. Click the cube once. An orange outline appears around it.
3. In the top menu, click **View** and make sure **Sidebar** has a tick. A panel opens on the right side of the viewport.
4. In the sidebar, find **Dimensions**. Each one says **2 m**.
5. Click in **Z**, type **6**, then press **Enter**. The cube stretches up.
6. Click in **X**, type **1.5**, press **Enter**. Do the same for **Y**.

- [ ] A tall, thin block stands in the middle of the screen
- [ ] The sidebar shows X 1.5 m, Y 1.5 m, Z 6 m

## Part 2 · Add loop cuts

[▶ Watch this part (0:55)](https://youtu.be/lxKNAoFo2Wk?t=55)

Loop cuts are rings that go around the column. They let you shape one section without changing the rest.

1. Move your mouse over the viewport and press **Tab**. The block goes into Edit Mode, and you see dots and lines on it.
2. In the toolbar on the left, click the **Loop Cut** tool.
3. Hover over a side of the column. A yellow ring appears. Only use rings that go **around** the column, not up and down.
4. Click to add a ring. Add about **five** near the top and **five** near the bottom.

- [ ] About ten rings go around the column, grouped near the top and bottom

## Part 3 · Remove a loop cut

[▶ Watch this part (1:36)](https://youtu.be/lxKNAoFo2Wk?t=96)

Everyone adds a ring in the wrong place sometimes. Here is how to fix it.

1. Add one extra ring on purpose in the middle.
2. At the top left of the viewport, click the **Edge Select** button (the middle of the three small square buttons).
3. Click the **Select Box** tool in the toolbar, then click one edge of the extra ring. Only the front edge turns orange.
4. In the top menu click **Select → Select Loops → Edge Loops**. Now the whole ring turns orange.
5. Right-click on the column and choose **Dissolve Edges**. The ring disappears.
6. If you want it back, use the **Loop Cut** tool again.

- [ ] The extra ring in the middle is gone
- [ ] The column still has its rings at the top and bottom

## Part 4 · Show tool names

[▶ Watch this part (2:26)](https://youtu.be/lxKNAoFo2Wk?t=146)

If your toolbar only shows small icons, you can make it show the names too.

1. Hover over the viewport and press **Tab** to go back to Object Mode. The dots and lines disappear.
2. Move your mouse to the right edge of the toolbar on the left. The pointer changes to a double arrow.
3. Drag to the right until you can read the tool names beside the icons.
4. Press **Tab** again to go back into Edit Mode. The edit tools appear with their names.

- [ ] The toolbar shows words like Select Box, Move, Scale and Loop Cut

## Part 5 · Squeeze a ring

[▶ Watch this part (2:57)](https://youtu.be/lxKNAoFo2Wk?t=177)

1. Select a whole ring using **Select → Select Loops → Edge Loops**, like in Part 3. The ring turns orange all the way around.
2. Click the **Scale** tool. Coloured handles appear on the ring.
3. Drag the **blue** handle part toward the centre. The ring pulls in and the column gets thinner there.

- [ ] One section of the column is skinnier than the rest

## Part 6 · Move a ring

[▶ Watch this part (3:16)](https://youtu.be/lxKNAoFo2Wk?t=196)

1. Use the **Loop Cut** tool to add a new ring near the bottom. Right after you add it, it is already selected (orange).
2. Click the **Move** tool. Red, green and blue arrows appear.
3. Drag the **blue** arrow. The ring slides only up or down.
4. To move a different ring, click one of its edges, then use **Select → Select Loops → Edge Loops** so the whole ring is selected before you move it.

- [ ] A ring has moved up or down, and the shape of the column changed

## Part 7 · Shape two rings at once

[▶ Watch this part (4:24)](https://youtu.be/lxKNAoFo2Wk?t=264)

1. Click one edge of a ring.
2. Hold **Shift** and click an edge of a second ring. Both edges turn orange.
3. Click **Select → Select Loops → Edge Loops**. Both rings are now selected all the way around.
4. To check, hold the **middle mouse button** and drag to spin your view. Both rings should be orange on every side.
5. Click the **Scale** tool and drag the blue handle part in. Both rings squeeze together.

- [ ] Two rings are orange all the way around
- [ ] Scaling changed both rings at the same time

## Part 8 · Make it fancy

[▶ Watch this part (6:28)](https://youtu.be/lxKNAoFo2Wk?t=388)

1. Add a few loop cuts very close together, then scale or move them to make bands and grooves.
2. To raise the top, click the **Face Select** button (the right of the three small square buttons).
3. Click the top face of the column. It turns orange.
4. Click the **Extrude Region** tool and drag the yellow handle up. A new piece grows from the top.

[▶ Review: removing a loop again (6:42)](https://youtu.be/lxKNAoFo2Wk?t=402)

- [ ] Your column has at least three different shaped sections
- [ ] The top has been extruded or shaped
